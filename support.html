Frequently asked

How do I make money?
Buy a truck or ship in Fleet, then use Build route to give it a repeating loop with a buy order in one city and a sell order in another. It trades automatically.

It runs on its own — can I close the app?
Yes. The world keeps trading while you are away, and a "Welcome back" summary shows what happened when you return.

Can I lose the game?
No. You can run out of cash, but you can never be permanently wiped out — take a loan or sell assets and rebuild.

Where are my saves?
Saves are stored on your own device only. The game saves automatically, and you can also keep named slots from the main menu (Load Game). Saves are not uploaded anywhere.

Is there an online mode, ads or purchases?
No. Tradewinds is fully offline with no ads, no in-app purchases, no account and no tracking.
